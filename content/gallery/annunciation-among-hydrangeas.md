Inspired by Piermatteo d'Amelia's *The Annunciation* at the Isabella Stewart Gardner museum, I painted a watercolor of my own design of the annunciation. Given to my grandma for her 82nd birthday. Her father, Giuseppe Liguori, had always encouraged her to pray the Rosary.

I think before the COVID-19 mayhem, the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston used to have a figure model come in every Wednesday. You were able to come in and sketch the figure model just by paying for your admission ticket. Unfortunately they don't do this anymore.

Ugh, we can't have anything nice for long in this city!

My friend came with me this day and we sketched the figure model together.

While out on a walk with my dog, a sudden rainstorm came and surprised us. We ran back home together, I think while I was listening to 'Aeroplane Over the Sea'. I had to run back outside because I dropped my phone in a puddle.

I sketched my wet dog in pen in the aftermath of the event.
